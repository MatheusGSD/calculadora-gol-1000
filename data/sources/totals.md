Findings:
- **No major agency or broadcaster uses Transfermarkt's lower Ronaldo number.** "974" appears only on an aggregator site. Transfermarkt's figure is mentioned only as a contrast ("TM/Sofascore show 961 while ESPN shows 967").
- The **Opta (The Analyst)** count, the **player's own count** (Instagram "900"), his club and the wire agencies (AFP, AP, PA, EFE) all include the Arab Club Champions Cup.
- FIFA.com and UEFA.com: we found no page stating a running total. One beIN mention that "FIFA communications" used the 950 count is **unverified**.
- We found no direct articles from Reuters, BBC, The Athletic, ge/Globo, Marca/AS or Record/A Bola (search returned syndicated copies only). This is a coverage gap. The numbers in the syndicated copies we did find follow the same count.

**Decision: the baseline is the press / Opta / player count, which equals messivsronaldo.app.**
- **Messi: 932.** Identical to Transfermarkt, so there are no per-match adjustments.
- **Ronaldo: 980** = Transfermarkt 974 + 6 Arab Club Champions Cup goals (2023). The press will declare "1,000" when Transfermarkt shows 994.

**Effect on the CSVs.** The six Arab Cup goals were all scored before the CSV window, which starts on 2024-07-01. So `running_career_total_after_match` in `cr7_matches.csv` equals the Transfermarkt running total + 6 on every row, with no per-match adjustment inside the window. Both counts are kept as separate columns: `running_career_total_after_match` (baseline) and `running_total_transfermarkt`.

## 6. Notes on the match-by-match CSVs (`messi_matches.csv`, `cr7_matches.csv`)
- **Scope.** Every official match the player's teams played from 2024-07-01 to 2026-10-10: club (Inter Miami / Al-Nassr) and national team (Argentina / Portugal), including matches the player missed.
  - Club matches come from the Transfermarkt match log, which lists every club match during his registration with a participation state. They were checked against the Transfermarkt team schedules, and no club match was missing.
  - National-team matches missing from the player's log were added from the team schedule. These are Portugal 2026-10-01 at Denmark and 2026-10-04 v Norway, after Ronaldo left the camp.
- **Excluded:** Argentina v Spain "Finalissima", 2026-03-27. UEFA cancelled it on 2026-03-15 and it was never played.
- **Dates and venue.**
  - `date` is the local match date: Eastern Time for matches in the Americas; for every other match the UTC kick-off falls on the same local date.
  - `home_away` uses N for tournament matches in a third country (Copa América 2024, Euro 2024, World Cup 2026), the Saudi Super Cup and the neutral-venue matches. Inter Miami's 2025 Club World Cup games are marked by real venue (`venue_note` column).
- **Status values:**
  - `played`, `unused_sub` (Transfermarkt "in squad"), `injured`, `suspended`.
  - `rested_or_not_in_squad`: rotation/rest, national-team duty, special leave, bereavement, Ronaldo's February 2026 boycott, leaving the Portugal camp.
  - `not_called_up`: Messi's two Argentina matches after his international retirement.
  - Every non-played row has a researched reason and a source URL (`reason_if_not_played`, from raw/absence_reasons.csv). Where only Transfermarkt's own absence field supports it, it is marked as such.
- **Extra columns** (after the requested ones): `running_total_transfermarkt`, `round`, `score_team_opp` (team score first; "(pens)" or "(aet)" where relevant), `reason_if_not_played`, `venue_note`, `tm_game_id`.
