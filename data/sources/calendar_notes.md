### 1.2 MLS Cup Playoffs 2026: format and dates
Source for this section: the official MLS announcement (https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/major-league-soccer-announces-audi-2026-mls-cup-playoffs-schedule), also reported at https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/mls-sets-2026-playoff-dates-192000977.html

| Round | Dates | Format |

| Round One, best of 3 | Game 1: 20–24 Nov (higher seed hosts)<br>Game 2: 27–28 Nov (lower seed hosts)<br>Game 3, if needed: 1–2 Dec (higher seed hosts) | Seeds 1–7 qualify directly, plus the two Wild Card winners. **Maximum playoff matches:**
- A top-7 seed plays at most 6 (3 + 1 + 1 + 1).
- An 8th or 9th seed plays at most 7.
- So Miami can play at most **7 regular-season + 6 playoff = 13** more official matches in 2026, or 14 if they drop to the Wild Card.

### 1.4 The 2027 calendar change (verified)
**The decision**
- MLS will switch to a **summer-to-spring calendar**. The Board of Governors ratified it in November 2025.
- The **2027–28** season starts in **July 2027** (mid-to-late July).
- There is a **mid-winter break from mid-December to early February**.
- **MLS Cup 2028 is in May 2028.**
- Sources: https://www.sandiegofc.com/news/here-s-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-2027-mls-calendar-shift ; https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/11/mls-announces-calendar-shift-moving-to-summer-to-spring-schedule-in-2027/ ; https://www.lagalaxy.com/mlsschedulechange

**The transition "Sprint Season" (spring 2027)**
- Announced 2026-03-19.
- **14 regular-season games per team**, all against conference opponents, 7 home and 7 away.
- Regular season runs roughly **February to April 2027**. **Playoffs and MLS Cup are in May 2027.**
- The **top 8 in each conference** make the playoffs.
- Its results decide qualification for the **2028** CONCACAF Champions Cup (5 MLS berths), the 2028 Leagues Cup (18 MLS clubs) and the 2028 U.S. Open Cup.
- Sources: https://www.goal.com/en/lists/mls-announces-details-for-shortened-sprint-season-amid-switch-to-fall-spring-calendar-in-2027/bltba1bd7246dc30aee ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2027_Major_League_Soccer_season (last edited 2026-09-19)

**Still missing for 2027**
- **MISSING:** exact start and end dates of the Sprint Season, its schedule, its playoff format, the number of playoff rounds, and the MLS Cup 2027 date. As of the 2026-03-19 announcement it "does not yet have a start and end date", and I found no fixture release.
- **MISSING:** the 2027–28 schedule and number of games. A 34-game season is expected but unconfirmed.

**Leagues Cup 2027**
- **MISSING / unconfirmed:** I found no announcement of a 2027 edition, either dates or format. The 2026 edition ran 4 Aug–6 Sep 2026 with 36 clubs (18 MLS + 18 Liga MX).
- Sources: https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/leagues-cup-2026-competition-dates-format-teams ; https://www.concacaf.com/news/leagues-cup-2026-schedule-unveiled

**CONCACAF Champions Cup 2027**
- 27 clubs. Four of them get a bye to the Round of 16, including the **MLS Cup 2026 winner**.
- MLS gets 5 berths, decided by the 2026 season:
  - MLS Cup winner
  - Supporters' Shield winner
  - Regular-season winner of the other conference
  - The next two clubs in the Supporters' Shield table
- **Miami has NOT qualified yet.** Currently around 6th overall, they would need to win MLS Cup 2026 or climb into the top Shield places.
- **MISSING:** CCC 2027 dates. Earlier editions ran February to June.
- Source: https://www.concacaf.com/competitions/champions-cup/news/2027-concacaf-champions-cup-qualified-clubs (2026-09-09)
