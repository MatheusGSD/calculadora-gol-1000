## 4. Post-World-Cup news (to 2026-10-10)

### Messi

- **Holiday after the World Cup.** He missed Inter Miami's matches on 2026-07-22 (Chicago, W 3-2) and 2026-07-25 (Montréal, W 1-0); Transfermarkt records these as "special leave". He and De Paul took a holiday of about 10 days in Argentina and returned to training on 2026-07-29 (https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/mls/articles/messi-returns-to-inter-miami-after-his-extraordinary-world-cup-2026-07-29). His first match back was 2026-08-01 against Columbus: he came on in the 53rd minute and the match ended 2-2 (https://africa.espn.com/football/story/_/id/49507355/lionel-messi-inter-miami-lineup-columbus-crew-mls-world-cup).
- **Father's death.** His father, Jorge Messi, died in Rosario on 2026-08-08 at the age of 68 (ESPN https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/49560549/jorge-messi-father-lionel-dies-aged-68; Euronews https://euronews.com/2026/08/13/i-really-doubt-ill-carry-on-messis-stark-words-in-farewell-to-his-father).
  - Messi was on compassionate leave and missed the Leagues Cup match against Monterrey on 2026-08-08, which Miami lost 1-2 (https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/574268.aspx).
  - In an open letter around 2026-08-12 he wrote: "now I have serious doubts about whether I'll keep doing it for much longer" (Buenos Aires Herald https://buenosairesherald.com/sports/stars/messi/lionel-messi-casts-doubts-on-football-future-following-fathers-death). This was not a retirement announcement.
- **International retirement (definitive).** He announced it on social media on 2026-08-31, calling it a "painful decision" that "comes at the right time" (QNA via The Peninsula https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/31/08/2026/lionel-messi-announces-international-retirement; Korea Times https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20260901/lionel-messi-says-retiring-from-international-football).
  - He was called up only for the farewell match (Al Jazeera https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/10/5/lionel-messi-argentina-farewell-final-match-benin). He was therefore not in the squad for the 2026-09-30 match against Bolivia or the 2026-10-03 match against Burkina Faso.
  - The farewell match was on 2026-10-06 against Benin at the Monumental. Argentina won 3-0. Messi played 90 minutes (Transfermarkt), scored a penalty in the 71st minute and made 2 assists. His final record for Argentina is 208 caps and 126 goals (ESPN https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50120909/lionel-messi-last-argentina-game-vs-benin-retirement).
  - **Consequence for the simulation:** Messi has no more national-team matches; all his future goals must come for Inter Miami.
- **Club retirement plans.** There is no announcement. Outlets say he intends to see out the Inter Miami contract (https://worldsoccertalk.com/news/is-lionel-messi-retiring-from-inter-miami-following-his-argentina-departure/). A report that he could retire at the end of 2026 is UNCONFIRMED; the outlet itself says there is no confirmation.
- **Inter Miami coach.**
  - Javier Mascherano stepped down in April 2026 for personal reasons, and Guillermo Hoyos took over as interim coach.
  - On 2026-08-27 the club named Cristian "Kily" González head coach, subject to a US work permit (https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/inter-miami-name-kily-gonzalez-head-coach).
  - Whether the work permit has since been approved is MISSING.

  - Matches missed (Transfermarkt status "not in squad"): the Saudi Pro League opener on 2026-08-15 against Al-Fateh and the King's Cup round of 32 on 2026-08-18 against Al-Diriyah.
  - Season debut: 2026-08-21 against Al-Riyadh, as a substitute for 28 minutes, with 1 goal.
- **Statements on retirement.**
  - Vogue interview, published by Reuters/Al Jazeera on 2026-08-16: "This is probably my last year of football and I want to leave a spectacular legacy" (https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/16/cristiano-ronaldo-hints-at-retirement-at-the-end-of-the-football-season).
  - On 2026-09-24, when his count stood at 979, he said: "Honestly, I don't know when I'm going to stop"; "I want to reach [1,000] and I will reach it, but it's not an obsession"; and that he would like the 1,000th goal to come in a national-team match (https://www.plataformamedia.com/en/2026/09/24/i-want-to-reach-it-and-i-will-reach-it-cristiano-ronaldo-1000-goals-target/).
  - He has not set a firm retirement date.
- **Portugal coach change.** Roberto Martínez left after the round-of-16 exit; his contract had expired. The FPF appointed Jorge Jesus on 2026-07-10 (AFP via https://www.onmanorama.com/sports/football/2026/07/10/portugal-appoint-jorge-jesus-as-head-coach-after-world-cup-exit.html). Jesus had been Ronaldo's coach at Al-Nassr and won the 2025-26 Saudi Pro League with them.
  - Ronaldo said he considered international retirement after the World Cup but chose to continue after talks with FPF president Pedro Proença and Jesus (Sky Sports https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13591141/cristiano-ronaldo-portugal-legend-considered-international-retirement-after-2026-world-cup-but-is-still-targeting-1-000-goals).

  - 2026-09-24 v Wales (W 1-0): started, 67 minutes, 0 goals.
  - 2026-09-27 v Norway (W 2-1): warmed up but was not used.
  - Before the Denmark match on 2026-10-01 he left the camp in Copenhagen. He also missed the 2026-10-04 return match against Norway.
  - On about 2026-10-06/07 he published a long statement (about 1,740 to 2,000 words depending on the outlet). In it he accused Jesus of twice breaking his word, apologised, asked to be punished, and said he is not retiring from the national team and will be "available" once any sanction has been served (The National https://www.thenationalnews.com/sport/football/2026/10/07/cristiano-ronaldo-jorge-jesus-portugal/; Sky Sports https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13595921/cristiano-ronaldo-portugal-star-breaks-silence-over-national-team-walkout-in-statement).
  - Jesus disputes the account (https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/uefa-nations-league/articles/dont-make-a-big-deal-out-of-it--jesus-shuts-down-reports-of-rift-with-ronaldo-2026-09-30).

  - Opened on 2026-10-09 and announced on 2026-10-10. It is classed as urgent.
  - Ronaldo is under a provisional suspension, first automatic and then non-automatic, until a final decision (ESPN https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/50146841/cristiano-ronaldo-suspended-portugal-national-team-walkout-jorge-jesus-disciplinary; France 24 https://www.france24.com/en/sport/20261010-portugal-hits-ronaldo-with-provisional-suspension-for-walking-out-on-national-team).
  - **Possible sanction:** the regulation allows a suspension of 1 to 6 months plus a fine. A full admission could halve this, so the minimum may be about 15 days (A Bola, via https://worldsoccertalk.com/news/cristiano-ronaldo-faces-crucial-portugal-verdict-as-disciplinary-hearing-date-emerges-but-suspension-could-be-cut-dramatically/).
  - **Scope:** the case concerns national-team duty. He played for Al-Nassr on 2026-10-09, so club football is not affected, although no article states the scope explicitly.
  - **Final sanction and length: MISSING** (pending).
  - Whether he will be available for the Nations League group matches on 2026-11-14 (v Denmark, home) and 2026-11-17 (v Wales, away) is **UNKNOWN**. It depends on the length of the sanction: a ban of one month or more from early October would rule him out.
